◆ identifyOrientationMarkers4()

bool calib::anonymous_namespace{fanuc_irvision_board.cpp}::identifyOrientationMarkers4 ( const std::vector< cv::Point2f > & m,
cv::Point2f & centre,
cv::Point2f & xMid,
cv::Point2f & xTip,
cv::Point2f & yTip )

Among the 4 large marker blobs (centre + 3 coord dots), identifies which is which purely from geometry: the centre/xMid/xTip triple is the one best fitting a straight line (smallest cross-product residual), leaving yTip as the off-line point; xMid is then picked out of the triple as the mid-distance point, and centre vs xTip are told apart by proximity to yTip. A final check rejects the fit if xMid isn't close to the midpoint of centre/xTip.

Parameters
mthe 4 marker blob centres, in arbitrary order
centreoutput; the identified centre-ring marker position
xMidoutput; the identified +X mid coord-dot position
xTipoutput; the identified +X tip coord-dot position
yTipoutput; the identified +Y coord-dot position
Returns
true if a consistent colinear triple + off-line point was found and passed the midpoint check
